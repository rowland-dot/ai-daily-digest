Today’s Editor’s Cut

The dominant story today isn’t a single model — it’s that the major labs are converging on the same competitive surface: context length. Anthropic shipped Claude 4.7 at 1M tokens, the Qwen team open-sourced an omnimodal checkpoint, and OpenAI quietly extended Responses-API context to 512K. Everyone is fighting for room to fit your codebase, your conversation history, and a few thousand lines of tool output simultaneously.

Meanwhile, the open-source side is still about cost. The two trajectories — frontier labs scaling context, open-source compressing inference — are converging on the same end-user: someone who used to think 32k was generous.

Six picks below. The Anthropic and Qwen launches are the can’t-miss ones; everything else is supporting context.
