Anthropic AIHOT Breaking 2h ago
★ Editor's Cut

Claude 4.7 launches with 1M context window

Anthropic shipped Claude 4.7 with a 1 million-token context window, available today on the API. Pricing matches the previous Opus tier.

Why it matters A 1M context window matters less for "fits a novel" and more for "fits a codebase and still has room for tool output and conversation history." The pricing parity is the real signal — Anthropic is betting context length, not raw intelligence, will be the differentiator this cycle.
